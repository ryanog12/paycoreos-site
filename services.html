 Chargeback Interception
Verifi — Visa CB Network
Visa · CDRN · Order Insight

Verifi is Visa's official chargeback interception network. When a Visa cardholder calls their bank to dispute a charge, Verifi alerts us first — giving you the opportunity to resolve it before it ever becomes a formal chargeback.

  • CDRN (Cardholder Dispute Resolution Network)
  • Order Insight — real-time transaction data sharing
  • Pre-dispute alerts from issuing banks
  • Stops chargebacks before they're filed
  • Protects your chargeback ratio
  • Helps avoid Visa fine programs (VAMP)
  • Works across all Visa-branded cards
Ethoca — Mastercard CB Network
Mastercard · Ethoca Alerts

Ethoca is Mastercard's chargeback prevention network. When a Mastercard cardholder disputes a transaction, Ethoca shares that data with merchants in real time — enabling resolution before a formal chargeback is processed.

  • Ethoca Alerts — real-time dispute notifications
  • Consumer Clarity — transaction data sharing
  • Intercepts Mastercard & Maestro disputes
  • Stops chargebacks before they hit your account
  • Reduces friendly fraud exposure
  • Helps avoid Mastercard MATCH listing
  • Works across all Mastercard-branded cards
Chargeback Ratio Management

  • Monthly ratio monitoring & reporting
