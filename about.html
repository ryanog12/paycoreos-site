Merchant Processing
Experts Since 2013.

PaycoreOS is a DBA of Telecom Elite, Inc. — a payment processing company built on over a decade of real-world experience. Starting in 2013 in telecommunications, we spent five years inside an international enterprise operation processing hundreds of merchant applications across high-risk and standard verticals with a 98% boarding success rate.

We specialize in two things most ISOs don't handle well: getting difficult merchants approved, and protecting their revenue through certified chargeback interception. As certified resellers for both Authorize.Net and NMI, and active participants in the Verifi (Visa) and Ethoca (Mastercard) chargeback networks, we provide the tools and expertise to keep your processing uninterrupted.

We know what processors look for, what raises red flags, and how to structure your application for approval. And once you're live, we actively defend your account health so you can focus on running your business — not fighting disputes.
